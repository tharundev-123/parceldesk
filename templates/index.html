🛡️ How OTP Handover Works

  1. Gate staff logs your incoming parcel and photos its condition.
  2. A single-use 6-digit code is generated and hashed with SHA-256.
  3. Show the OTP to the gate guard when collecting.
  4. The guard enters it into ParcelDesk to release your package.
